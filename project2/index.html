About the Store

Outdoor Supply Co. was founded in 2018 and its purpose is to make it easier for people to find trustworthy equipment that they may need when hiking. The idea of opening an outdoor equipment store came from a love of hiking and spending time outdoors. This store is designed to help customers find exactly what they need for any hiking adventure. Outdoor Supply Co. offers a variety of essential hiking equipment, including shoes, base layers, mid layers, outer layers, pants, and trekking poles. All of our employees are experienced in a variety of outdoor activities and are there to personally help customers find the right equipment and get properly fitted for their next adventure.
